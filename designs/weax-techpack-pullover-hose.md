# WEAX – Tech Pack Prompt: Pullover + Hose

Gleicher Aufbau wie der Tech Pack für T-Shirt + Shorts – nur als Pullover (Hoodie) + Hose. Logo in Gemini mit hochladen, dann den ganzen Block einfügen.

```
Create a premium, monochrome streetwear fashion tech pack on a deep black background, presenting a matching black hoodie (pullover) and wide-leg pants set in a professional luxury clothing design layout.

The presentation must look like a high-end fashion designer's technical product sheet, with realistic fabric textures, front and back views, detailed close-ups, and precise material specifications.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. PULLOVER (HOODIE) DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Oversized black pullover hoodie
- Double-layer hood with a clean edge
- NO drawstrings, NO cords, NO metal eyelets on the hood
- NO zipper
- Relaxed, premium streetwear fit with slightly dropped shoulders
- One clean kangaroo pocket on the front
- Two flowing, curved wavy stripes running vertically along both sides
- Matching wavy stripes continuing along the sleeves
- ALL STRIPES MUST BE MEDIUM GRAY ONLY
- No white stripes, no silver stripes, no gradients, and no multicolored stripe effects
- Ribbed sleeve cuffs and ribbed bottom hem, each with a thin gray double stripe
- Small embroidered metallic silver-white logo on the left chest
- The logo features an elegant stylized W, an elliptical orbital ring looping around the letters, and a sharp eight-pointed star above the right side
- Realistic heavyweight cotton fleece texture
- Front and back views displayed side by side
- No logo on the back of the hoodie
- Preserve the exact same stripe design, shape, and positioning throughout

PULLOVER FABRIC SPECIFICATIONS:

- Material composition: 100% cotton
- Fabric type: heavyweight brushed-back cotton fleece
- Fabric weight: 400 GSM
- Acceptable production range: 380–420 GSM
- Soft, warm, brushed inside
- Smooth, clean outer surface with natural cotton texture
- Structured, premium drape that holds the oversized shape
- Ribbing: 1x1 cotton rib with a small amount of elastane for recovery
- No polyester, nylon, or other synthetic fibers in the main fabric

The hoodie should look heavy, warm and premium, with enough structure to display the gray stripes beautifully.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. PANTS DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Matching black cotton pants
- Relaxed, wide-leg streetwear fit
- Full length, falling straight and stacking slightly over the shoes
- Clean, minimal silhouette
- Elastic waistband
- NO drawstrings
- NO cords
- NO hanging strings
- Wide, open leg hems – NO ribbed ankle cuffs, NO elastic at the ankles
- Two side pockets hidden in the side seams
- One clean, rectangular back pocket
- Two flowing, curved wavy stripes running down both outer sides of the legs
- ALL PANTS STRIPES MUST BE MEDIUM GRAY ONLY
- The gray stripe color must match the hoodie exactly
- Small embroidered metallic silver-white logo on the front of the right thigh
- Matching embroidered logo centered on the back pocket
- Front and back views displayed side by side

PANTS FABRIC SPECIFICATIONS:

- Material composition: 100% cotton
- Fabric type: heavyweight cotton French terry
- Fabric weight: 380 GSM
- Acceptable production range: 360–400 GSM
- Soft and comfortable, warm but not bulky
- Substantial enough to keep the wide-leg shape and a clean straight fall
- Smooth outer surface with subtle cotton texture
- No polyester, nylon, or other synthetic fibers in the main fabric

The pants should look relaxed, premium and heavy enough to hold their wide-leg shape.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. EXACT LOGO DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use the same logo design consistently across the hoodie and pants.

The logo consists of:

- Elegant, stylized W lettering
- A smooth elliptical orbital ring crossing around the letters
- A sharp eight-pointed star positioned above the upper-right portion
- Metallic silver-white embroidered appearance
- Fine, realistic embroidery texture
- Small, refined luxury branding
- Consistent proportions and appearance across all logo placements

The logo must look embroidered into the fabric, not printed.

Do not redesign, simplify, distort, or replace the logo.

The front hoodie logo, front pants logo, and back pocket logo must all use the same design.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. STRICT COLOR REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Garment color:
Deep matte black.

Stripe color:
Medium gray only.

Logo color:
Metallic silver-white.

Background:
Nearly black.

Typography:
White and light gray.

Fabric swatches:
Black, medium gray, and white.

IMPORTANT:

All decorative wavy stripes on the hoodie and pants must be gray.

The stripes must never be white or silver-white.

Do not add white outlines around the gray stripes.

Do not use gradients or multiple shades within the stripes.

The gray stripes must have the same color and visual appearance on the sleeves, side panels, legs, front views, back views, and close-up details.

The logos are the only garment graphics that may be metallic silver-white.

Preserve the flowing, curved stripe pattern exactly.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. FABRIC AND GSM DETAIL PANELS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Include a vertical column of framed close-up details on the right side of the presentation.

Show realistic close-ups of:

1. Double-layer hood without drawstrings
2. Embroidered silver-white W orbit-and-star logo
3. Medium-gray side stripes
4. Ribbed sleeve cuff with gray double stripe
5. Brushed fleece inside of the hoodie
6. Elastic pants waistband without drawstrings
7. Back pocket with embroidered logo
8. Wide open pants hem

Include separate fabric specification labels with the following exact text:

PULLOVER:
“100% COTTON”
“HEAVYWEIGHT BRUSHED FLEECE”
“400 GSM”
“WARM AND STRUCTURED”
“NATURAL FIBERS”

PANTS:
“100% COTTON”
“HEAVYWEIGHT COTTON FRENCH TERRY”
“380 GSM”
“WIDE-LEG FIT”
“SOFT AND DURABLE”

Make the cotton texture look realistic, with fine natural fibers and premium-quality stitching.

The fabric must look heavy, soft and premium, not synthetic or shiny.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. TECH PACK LAYOUT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a professional fashion designer's technical presentation.

Layout:

- Hoodie front and back views in the upper half
- Pants front and back views in the lower half
- Three circular fabric color swatches arranged vertically on the left
- A vertical column of framed construction details on the right
- Thin gray divider lines around the detail panels
- Clean uppercase technical typography
- Small, precise material labels
- Balanced spacing and symmetrical composition
- Consistent garment proportions
- Realistic stitching, seams, cuffs, and pockets

Use a deep black background with subtle studio lighting to emphasize the cotton texture.

The entire presentation should resemble a premium clothing manufacturer's technical design sheet.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. VISUAL STYLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Luxury streetwear aesthetic
- Photorealistic garment visualization
- Professional fashion product photography
- Deep black background
- High contrast with soft studio lighting
- Realistic fabric folds and heavy cotton texture
- Detailed embroidery
- Precise gray stripe placement
- Clean, minimal composition
- Sophisticated monochrome branding
- No people
- No mannequins
- No unnecessary decorations

At the bottom of the presentation, include:

“MINIMAL / CLEAN / TIMELESS”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. FINAL REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Both garments must be made from 100% cotton.

Hoodie:
400 GSM heavyweight brushed-back cotton fleece.

Pants:
380 GSM heavyweight cotton French terry.

Keep the oversized hoodie silhouette and the relaxed, full-length wide-leg pants.

The hoodie has no drawstrings and no zipper.

The pants have an elastic waistband without drawstrings and wide open leg hems.

All wavy stripes must be medium gray only.

All embroidered logos must remain metallic silver-white.

Preserve the original W orbit-and-star logo.

Maintain consistent logo sizes and stripe designs across all views.

Do not introduce polyester blends.

Do not change the garment design, stripe pattern, or logo.

The final result must be a highly detailed, realistic, premium streetwear tech pack suitable for presenting the clothing design to a professional manufacturer.
```
